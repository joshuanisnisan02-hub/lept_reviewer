# Source research baseline

## Primary PRC references

1. Professional Regulatory Board for Professional Teachers Resolution No. 11, s. 2025
   - Amends Resolution No. 11, s. 2022 on the Enhanced Table of Specifications for LEPT.
   - This should be treated as a primary source for the current examination blueprint.

2. Professional Regulatory Board for Professional Teachers Resolution No. 20, s. 2025
   - Alignment of the Commission on Higher Education teacher education curriculum and LEPT.
   - Use together with its annex when building program/specialization mapping.

3. PRC Resolution No. 2053(A), s. 2025
   - Guidelines on implementation of PRC-CHED alignment for LEPT.

## Build rule

Before seeding production modules/questions:
- extract the exact current PRC exam areas and competencies;
- preserve TOS weighting and cognitive-level distributions where stated;
- link each competency to its primary source;
- only then create modules, lessons, flashcards, and original practice items;
- keep AI-generated items in Draft/For Review until human verification.
